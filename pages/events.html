Syncing on an iPhone or iPad

Tapping Subscribe (Apple / Outlook) should open the Calendar app directly and offer to add the PTA calendar automatically. If it doesn't:

  1. Open Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other.
  2. Tap Add Subscribed Calendar.
  3. Paste this URL: webcal://calendar.google.com/calendar/ical/729fff6a3c9b55b25ec82db33400efd852520f8b39f1386e617ceb9848c818b0%40group.calendar.google.com/public/basic.ics

Once subscribed this way, new events the PTA adds show up automatically — no need to re-import.
