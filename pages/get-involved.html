Join Us

Becoming a member is the main thing we ask, and it's the only thing we ask. Attending meetings or volunteering is always completely optional.

Membership

Become a Member

Joining Thunder Hill Elementary PTA is how you support Thunder Hill — full stop. Your membership alone already helps cover the cost of the programs we fund, and it also gives you a vote at meetings and priority for volunteer sign-ups, if you'd ever like to use them.

Join via Membership Portal

Want to Do More?

A few ways to pitch in, whenever it works for you.

Classroom Helper

Read with students, help at parties, or assist teachers during the school day.

Event Volunteer

Set up, run a table, or clean up at picnics, fairs, and fundraisers.

Committee Member

Join a standing committee — fundraising, hospitality, or communications.

Run for the Board

A bigger commitment with a bigger impact — elections happen every spring.
