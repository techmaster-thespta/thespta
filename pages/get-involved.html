Get Involved

There's a Place for Every Family

No time commitment is too small. Whether you have five minutes or five hours a month, there's a way to pitch in.

Classroom Helper

Read with students, help at parties, or assist teachers during the school day.

Event Volunteer

Set up, run a table, or clean up at picnics, fairs, and fundraisers.

Committee Member

Join a standing committee — fundraising, hospitality, or communications.

Run for the Board

A bigger commitment with a bigger impact — elections happen every spring.

Membership

Become a Member

Membership is $10 per family, per school year and includes a vote at general meetings, member-only volunteer sign-ups, and a say in how funds are spent. Everyone is welcome at events and to volunteer whether or not you're a member.
